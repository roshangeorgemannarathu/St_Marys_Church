Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception

Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception is the patroness of purity and grace. The title refers to Mary being conceived without original sin, chosen by God to be the mother of Jesus Christ.

Her devotion inspires the faithful to live a life of holiness. Her immaculate heart shines as a symbol of unconditional love and divine intercession.

St. Thomas Cross

The St Thomas Cross is the symbol used by the St. Thomas Christian community in India, traditionally linked to the Apostle Thomas.

The empty cross represents the resurrected Christ, while the descending dove and lotus flower beautifully symbolize the Holy Trinity.
